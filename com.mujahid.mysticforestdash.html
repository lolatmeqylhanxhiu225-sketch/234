Privacy Policy

Welcome to PSCI Pro. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you use our application on your Android device.

Data Collection Statement

PSCI Pro does not collect, store, or upload any user data. This application is designed to function locally without requiring your personal information to be transmitted to external servers.

No Personal Information

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information (PII). We do not require user registration to utilize the core features of the application.

Usage Analytics & Tracking

  • No Analytics SDKs: The application does not contain third-party analytics tools (such as Google Firebase Analytics or Amplitude).
  • No Advertising SDKs: The application does not track user behavior for advertising purposes.
  • No Crashing Logs: We do not collect or log crash reports or user session logs.

Permissions & Privacy

Our application respects your device permissions. As we do not process or upload data, we have requested the minimum permissions necessary to ensure the application functions correctly on the Android operating system (e.g., storage access solely for local file management, if applicable). No location data, contact lists, or microphone usage is requested.

Third-Party Services

This application operates independently and does not integrate with external third-party services that would compromise user privacy. While the application icon is loaded from a remote source for display purposes, this does not constitute data transmission to third-party servers.

Children's Privacy

The application is not intended for children under 13. As no data is collected, the application is inherently safe for all users, but it is not marketed or designed specifically for young children.

Changes to This Policy

While we are committed to not collecting your data, our Privacy Policy may change in the future if we introduce new features that require data handling. Any updates will be displayed on this page with a new "Last updated" date. By continuing to use the application, you agree to any changes.
